Welcome to Mulligan Masters

Great People, Good Golf, Amazing Vibes - This is the mission of Mulligan Masters of the Bay. A local golf group that was founded by Amit and Jackie to bring together passionate golfers they met over time into a fun, welcoming and laid-back environment.

What We Offer

Fun Tournaments

Regular events for all skill levels.

Strong Community

A welcoming group of golf enthusiasts who share tips, stories, and friendship.

Improvement

Opportunities to enhance your skills through practice sessions and friendly advice.

Join the League

Email Us

Send an email to mulliganmastersofthebay@gmail.com with the following:

  • A short bio including:
    • Who you are
    • Your location
    • Your handicap
    • How often you play 18
    • What you're hoping to get out of the group

Guest Group Chat

Once reviewed, you'll be added to the Mulligan Masters Guest Chat, where we share tee times and plan rounds.

Become a Mulligan Master

After attending 7+ meetups as a guest, you could be invited into the core MM group chat and officially become a Mulligan Master.

Code of Conduct

We reserve the right to remove anyone from the guest or main group if they don't align with the MM code.
