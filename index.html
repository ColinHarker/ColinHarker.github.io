Welcome to the league

A golf group built around the people, not the score.

Mulligan Masters of the Bay was founded by Amit and Jackie to bring together passionate golfers across the Bay Area — in a fun, welcoming, laid-back environment that prizes camaraderie over leaderboards.

We play the courses we love, host events that mean something, and welcome new faces every season.

Become a member

Join the league.

We're a small, intentional group. New members come in through a guest period — here's how it works.

01

Email us

Send an introduction to mulliganmastersofthebay@gmail.com with a short bio:

  • Who you are and where you're based
  • Your handicap and how often you play 18
  • What you're hoping to get out of the group
02

Guest group chat

Once reviewed, you'll be added to the Mulligan Masters Guest Chat — where we share tee times and plan rounds.

03

Become a Mulligan Master

After attending 7+ meetups as a guest, you may be invited into the core MM group chat and officially become a Mulligan Master.

04

Code of conduct

We reserve the right to remove anyone from the guest or main group if they don't align with the MM code — great people first, always.
