Welcome to Mulligan Masters

Great People, Good Golf, Amazing Vibes - This is the mission of Mulligan Masters of the Bay. A local golf group that was founded by Amit and Jackie to bring together passionate golfers they met over time into a fun, welcoming and laid-back environment.

What We Offer

Fun Tournaments

Regular events for all skill levels.

Strong Community

A welcoming group of golf enthusiasts who share tips, stories, and friendship.

Improvement

Opportunities to enhance your skills through practice sessions and friendly advice.

Join the League

  1. Send us an email at mulliganmastersofthebay@gmail.com with the following information:

    • A short bio about you detailing:
      • Who you are
      • Your location
      • Your Handicap
      • How often do you play 18
      • What are you expecting from joining this group

  2. Once we review, you will be added to the Mulligan Masters Guest Group Chat, where tee times will be shared.

  3. After you attend 7 MM meetups, you will get pulled into the main core group chat and become a MM.

  4. The MM group reserves the right to remove a member from the guest group or the main group if they don't abide by the MM code.
