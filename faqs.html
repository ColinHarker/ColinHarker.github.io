FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers about the group, membership, events, and how to get involved.

What is Mulligan Masters of the Bay?

Mulligan Masters is a Northern California-based golf group focused on bringing good people together to enjoy the game of golf in a relaxed, fun, and spirited environment. We welcome golfers of all skill levels.

Who can join Mulligan Masters?

Anyone who shares our love for golf, good company, and friendly competition is welcome. While many members are based in the Bay Area, we welcome anyone willing to travel and participate.

How do I become a member?

Membership is by invitation or referral from an existing member. If you're interested in joining, feel free to contact us through the Contact page to learn more.

How often do you play?

We play year-round, with monthly events and special tournaments throughout the season. Our schedule includes casual rounds, competitive events, and the occasional golf trip.

Is there a handicap requirement to join?

Not at all. We have golfers ranging from scratch to beginners. What matters most is a great attitude, respect for the game, and a willingness to have fun.

What are your events like?

Our events range from relaxed weekend rounds to organized tournaments with prizes, custom swag, photography, and post-round gatherings. Every event is designed to foster connection, laughter, and a few memorable mulligans.

Can I bring a guest?

Yes, members can often invite guests to select events—especially if they're considering joining the group. Just check with the event organizer in advance.

Do you have a social media presence?

YES!! We're currently building out our Instagram page to share event highlights, photos, and member stories. Follow us on @mulliganmasters

Who runs the group?

The group is member-driven, with a few core organizers coordinating events, communication, and the overall vibe. It's a community effort, and everyone contributes to the good times.

Does it cost anything to be a part of the league?

No, there's no cost to join the league. We're all about bringing good people together to enjoy the game—no membership fees, no pressure.
