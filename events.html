August 23, 2025

Golf for Good Scramble

Poppy Ridge Golf Course

The Mulligan Masters came together for a day of golf, community, and giving back at the beautiful Poppy Ridge Golf Course in Livermore. This fun-focused scramble tournament brought together golfers of all skill levels to support the Peninsula Humane Society & SPCA while enjoying one of the East Bay's premier courses.

With stunning views, challenging holes, and great company, the day was filled with memorable moments both on and off the course. Proceeds from the event went directly to helping animals in need, making every birdie and eagle that much sweeter.

April 27, 2025

Monterey Bay

Poppy Hill Golf Course

The Mulligan Masters hit the fairways of Poppy Hills Sunday April 27th 2025 for an unforgettable round in the heart of Pebble Beach. With 20 golfers teeing off in their Argyle Fashion, the course offered its signature blend of beauty and challenge, surrounded by towering pines and rolling coastal terrain. Spirits were high, competition was friendly, and the camaraderie was as strong as ever.

We were lucky to have a professional photographer on hand capturing the day—from pre-round laughs to clutch putts and scenic moments. The photos perfectly reflect the heart of what this group is all about: good people, great golf, and even better memories. From smooth swings to creative recoveries, every hole had a story. March 15, 2025

Northwood

Northwood golf course

The Mulligan Masters made their way to the redwoods for a special round at Northwood Golf Club, one of the most unique tracks in Northern California. Tucked along the Russian River and framed by towering redwoods, the course delivered a peaceful yet tricky nine-hole challenge. The vibe was laid-back but the shot-making was serious, with tight fairways demanding accuracy and creativity.

Laughter echoed through the trees as groups looped the course twice, enjoying the old-school charm and natural beauty of Northwood. It was a day that reminded us why we play—great company, good competition, and a setting you can't beat. Browse our gallery to relive the round and experience the magic of golf beneath the redwoods.
