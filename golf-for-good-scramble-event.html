Tournament Purpose

  • Connect with like-minded golf enthusiasts and Bay Area professionals.
  • Support the local Peninsula Humane Society & SPCA and their mission to protect and care for animals in need.
  • Enjoy a non-competitive, fun-focused scramble with great people on a brand-new course.

Scramble Format & Rules

  • Team Format: 4-player teams (scramble style)
  • Tee Shots: All players tee off on each hole; team selects the best shot
  • Subsequent Shots: All players then play from that chosen spot
  • Completion: Continue until the ball is holed
  • Drives: No minimum drives required—pure fun, not pressure
  • Mulligans: May be available for donation
