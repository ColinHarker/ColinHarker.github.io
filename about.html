About Mulligan Masters

Our Story

Great People, Good Golf, Amazing Vibes - This is the mission of Mulligan Masters of the Bay. A local golf group that was founded by Amit and Jackie to bring together passionate golfers they met over time into a fun, welcoming and laid-back environment.

What started as a small group of friends has grown into a vibrant golf community of 30+ members who share a passion for the game. Whether you're a seasoned player or just getting into golf, our group strives to provide a space where like-minded individuals can connect, improve, and enjoy the sport without the pressure of a traditional club.

We organize regular rounds at some of the best courses in Northern California, friendly competitions, and social gatherings that extend beyond the fairways. More than just a golf league, Mulligan Masters of the Bay is about building deep friendships, exploring new courses, and creating unforgettable experiences on and off the course.

Our Mission

At Mulligan Masters, our mission is to foster a welcoming community of golf enthusiasts who support each other in improving their skills, enjoying the game, and building lasting friendships.

We believe that golf is more than just a sport—it's a way to connect with others, challenge yourself, and enjoy the beauty of nature. Our group is committed to making golf accessible and enjoyable for everyone, regardless of experience or skill level.

Our Values

Comaraderie

Comaraderie Over Competition.

We prioritize friendship, laughter, and shared memories over winning. Every round is about connecting, not just competing.

Respect

Respect for the Game and Each Other

Whether you're a scratch golfer or just starting out, we honor the spirit of golf and treat each other with encouragement, patience, and integrity.

Passion

Play With Passion, Not Pressure

We're here to relax, have fun, and share the passion of the game. Every mulligan is a chance to enjoy the moment, not stress the score.
