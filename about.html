About Mulligan Masters

Our Story

Mulligan Masters was founded by a group of passionate golfers who wanted to create a community where players of all skill levels could come together, improve their game, and enjoy the sport in a friendly, supportive environment.

What started as a small group of friends has grown into a vibrant golf community of members and guests who share a passion for the game. Whether you're a seasoned player or just getting into golf, our group strives to provide a space where like-minded individuals can connect, improve, and enjoy the sport without the pressure of a traditional club.

Our Mission

At Mulligan Masters, our mission is to foster a welcoming community of golf enthusiasts who support each other in improving their skills, enjoying the game, and building lasting friendships.

We believe that golf is more than just a sport—it's a way to connect with others, challenge yourself, and enjoy the beauty of nature. Our group is committed to making golf accessible and enjoyable for everyone, regardless of experience or skill level.

Our Values

Sportsmanship

We value fair play, respect for the game, and courtesy toward fellow players. Good sportsmanship is at the heart of everything we do.

Community

We believe in building a supportive community where members can share experiences, learn from each other, and form lasting friendships.

Growth

We encourage continuous improvement, both in golf skills and personal development, in a supportive and non-judgmental environment.

Join Us

We're always excited to welcome new members to the Mulligan Masters community. Whether you're a seasoned golfer or just starting out, there's a place for you in our group.

Joining is simple—just reach out to us via email, and we'll provide you with information about upcoming events and how to get involved. We look forward to meeting you on the course!
